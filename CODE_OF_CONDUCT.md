# Community conduct

Discuss code and evidence respectfully. Critique an implementation rather than
the person who proposed it. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation
and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

Keep discussions relevant and reproducible. Do not share credentials, private
saves or game/BIOS material you are not entitled to distribute. Security reports
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue comments.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict
participation. Explain moderation decisions when doing so does not disclose private
information. Request a private contact channel without posting sensitive details
if a concern cannot safely be discussed in the issue tracker.
